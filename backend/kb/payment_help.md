# Payment and Auto-Debit Support Policies

## Auto-Debit Failure and Bounce Charge Waiver Policy
When an automated NACH or e-Mandate auto-debit attempt fails due to insufficient balance or technical clearing errors, partnered lending institutions may levy a standard bounce fee of ₹350 to ₹500. Under Paytm lending assistance guidelines, borrowers who clear the outstanding installment within a 48-hour grace window from the scheduled debit date are eligible for a 100% waiver of this bounce charge. Borrowers must use the verified instant settlement link generated in the app to qualify for the waiver.

## Payment Reconciliation and Double Debit Protocol
In case a payment is deducted from the borrower's bank account or UPI handle but does not reflect on the loan dashboard, an automated inter-bank reconciliation token is initiated immediately. Transactions under bank processing undergo automated NPCI clearing. If the funds do not reflect within 2 hours, the amount is automatically refunded back to the source bank account within 24 to 48 banking hours. Borrowers receive an immutable grievance tracking ticket ensuring zero duplicate penalties.

## Financial Hardship and Loan Restructuring Guidelines
Borrowers encountering genuine temporary income disruption, medical emergencies, or employment transition can request sample loan restructuring assistance. The restructuring framework permits a tenure extension of up to 6 months, which reduces the immediate monthly EMI obligation by up to 25%. A temporary moratorium of up to 90 days on principal repayment may also be evaluated by lending partners, subject to document verification and credit committee consent.

## Instant Repayment Link and Digital Settlement
Borrowers experiencing UPI mandate failure can execute repayment using verified alternative channels including debit cards, net banking, or Paytm UPI handle. The instant repayment token ensures immediate ledger updating, halting automated collection reminders and preventing negative credit bureau reporting during the active dispute resolution cycle.
